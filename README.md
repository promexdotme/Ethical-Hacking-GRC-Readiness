## Executive Summary
In the cybersecurity industry, the difference between a highly paid professional and a target of an FBI federal indictment is often a single document: **The Paper Trail.** 

This repository serves as my Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) portfolio. It demonstrates my ability to navigate the strict legal boundaries of penetration testing, including the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA) and state cybercrime laws. 

Understanding a Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (VDP) is critical. A VDP acts as the legal "Rules of Engagement" between a company (or government) and a hacker. It defines the "Safe Harbor" that protects researchers from prosecution, and the "Out of Scope" red lines that, if crossed, revoke all legal protections. Professionals read the VDP to stay out of jail; criminals ignore it and face the consequences.

## Repository Organization
This repository contains the required legal and governance documents to execute a fully authorized penetration test:

*   **📁 Phase_1_Legal_Risk_Brief:** Contains the legal risk breakdown of CFAA vs. State laws and indemnification concepts.
*   **📁 Phase_2_Governance_Kit:** Contains a mock Rules of Engagement (RoE) and a formal Permission to Test (PTT) authorization letter.
*   **📁 Phase_3_Policy_Audit:** Contains an audit report of the actual U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) VDP.
